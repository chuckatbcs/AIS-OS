# Voice Reference

Match this register when drafting. Don't fake Chuck's voice on external content without showing a draft first.

## Sample 1 — Email to customer

> "Can you sign this and send a copy of your driver license back to me in order to transfer the Spectrum account to your control?  I already reduced the bill to $60/month, but the card info is expiring this month and you will need to update it."

## Sample 2 — Informal chat

> "You can use this chat and past chats to see how i talk informally to agents and in email."

## Register notes

- Casual but professional
- Short sentences
- No em dashes
- Bullet points over paragraphs
- Direct, no fluff
- Lead with what needs action
- Uses contractions naturally
- Lowercase "i" in casual contexts
